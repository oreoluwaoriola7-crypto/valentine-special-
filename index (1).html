Chapter I  ·  The Man

Oladimeji

Sometimes words cannot fully express how great you are — as an individual, as a person, and as my friend. But on this Valentine’s Day, I will try.

There is something rare about you, Dimeji. It is the way you carry yourself — with quiet confidence and a warmth that makes everyone around you feel seen, heard, and valued.

You are the kind of person who walks into a room and somehow makes it brighter without even trying. Your character is not performative — it is genuine. Your kindness is not occasional — it is who you are.

I have watched you handle life with a grace that many people spend years trying to learn. You give without counting the cost, you show up without being asked, and you love without conditions. That is a gift the world does not deserve, but is lucky to have.

Chapter II  ·  An Ode

To Oladimeji

A letter from the heart

✦
You are not just a name I call
when laughter is needed or walls begin to fall —
you are the constant in the equation,
the anchor beneath every occasion.
In a world that moves too fast to feel,
you reminded me what’s tender, what’s real.
Not once did you make a moment feel small —
you brought your whole self to all of it all.
They say some people pass through your life like seasons —
beautiful but brief, gone without reasons.
But you, Dimeji, are the kind that stays —
a rare and golden soul wrapped in quiet ways.
So here is what I want you to know today:
You are deeply appreciated in every way.
Not just for what you do — but for who you are.
You are, without question, my favourite star.

✦   With deep admiration   ✦

Chapter III  ·  Us

Two Brothers,
One Story

There is a special kind of friendship that does not need grand gestures to prove itself — it shows up in the ordinary moments, in the laughter that needs no explanation, in the silences that are never uncomfortable.

That is what we have, Dimeji.

Every memory we have shared is one I hold close — not just because it was fun, but because you were in it. You have been a brother to me in every sense that matters, and then some.

On this Valentine’s Day, I want you to know that I celebrate you — not just your achievements, not just your good days, but all of you, in every season.

Thank you for being my person. Here is to every chapter still ahead of us.

Happy Valentine’s Day, Dimeji.  ❤
